Metro Lines

All of Metro's stations, yards, and operations sorted by line.

Red Line

Opened March 27th, 1976. Serves 27 stations from Shady Grove to Glenmont.

Blue Line

Opened July 1st, 1977. Serves 28 stations from Franconia-Springfield to Downtown Largo.

Orange Line

Opened November 20th, 1978. Serves 26 stations from Vienna to New Carrollton.

Yellow Line

Opened April 30th, 1983. Serves 22 stations from Huntington to Greenbelt.

Green Line

Opened May 11th, 1991. Serves 21 stations from Branch Ave to Greenbelt.

Silver Line

Opened July 26th, 2014. Serves 39 stations from Ashburn to Downtown Largo/New Carrollton.
