About

The 5000-Series were the first Metro railcars of the new millennium and with that came some changes. They are notable for being the first Metro cars to feature LED destination signs, a red, white & blue interior, and interior next-stop signs. When first introduced, all text on the electornic signage was typed in lowercase, with only the first letters capitalized. The text would be changed to all uppercase shortly after their introduction, to match the flip-dot signage present on the rest of Metro's fleet. The 5Ks also feature unique bulkhead windows with large window frames in either silver, black, or brown.

Construction

In May 1998, WMATA awarded a $212 million contract to Construcciones y Auxiliar de Ferrocarriles (CAF), in partnership with AAI Corporation, for the construction of 192 new railcars set for the new millennium. The car bodies were constructed at CAF's plants in Beasain, Spain & Elmira Heights, NY. They would be transported to AAI Corp's factory in Hunt Valley, MD for final assembly. From there, the railcars would be delivered by truck to WMATA's Greenbelt Yard. They entered service on the Green Line on August 23rd, 2001 with a ceremonial ribbon cutting held at Greenbelt station.

Retirement

After facing a multitude of issues, the 5Ks would be sent for an early retirement when WMATA ordered an additional 220 7000-Series railcars to replace them. On October 12th, 2018, WMATA announced all the 5Ks would be retired the following day but were spotted in service through the following week. Scrapping would be contracted to RemacUSA in Sparrows Point, MD, beginning on October 3rd, 2018, with cars 5050-5051, and ending the week of July 5th, 2019, with cars 5188-5189. Following their retirement, cars 5112-5113 were set aside and held for donation to an unknown transit agency. The donation fell through and the pair would be scrapped on December 12th, 2019.

Present Day

Only two pairs of 5000-Series railcars survived post-retirement. Cars 5000-5001 are preserved by WMATA and stored in Greenbelt Yard. Cars 5058 & 5059 were purchased by a private investor in 2019, with 5058 becoming metrobar DC while 5059 was moved to a private property in Halethorpe, MD with no immediate plans for it's future.
