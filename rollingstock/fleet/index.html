Metrorail's Fleet

On May 3rd, 1972, WMATA awarded a $91.6 million contract to Rohr Industries to construct and deliver the first 300 Metro railcars. These cars would be known as the 1000-Series (1Ks) and were constructed at Rohr’s plant in Winder, Georgia.
