About

On March 17th, 2021, WMATA awarded an up to $2.2 billion contract to Hitachi Rail to construct a base order of 256 cars, with options for up to 800 cars. The base order would allow Metro to replace all 2000-Series & 3000-Series railcars. In 2022, Hitachi Rail would announce the construction of a new facility in Hagerstown, MD which would be used to construct the 8000-Series fleet. On March 20th, 2024, the first 8K soft mockup would debut to the public, displayed on the National Mall. The soft mockup would feature an open gangway and blue/black stripe livery. A livery vote would be announced on August 18th, 2025, with three options, all featuring a change to a brown/gray color scheme. The winner would be revealed on August 27th, a brown stripe broken up by gray 'M's. A two-car hard mockup featuring the new livery would be displayed along the National Mall beginning on June 25th, 2026.

Construction

The 8000-Series will be built at Hitachi Rail's new facility in Hagerstown, MD beginning in 2027. The first cars are scheduled to be delivered in late 2027 and enter service in 2028.
