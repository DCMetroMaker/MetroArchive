Incidents

Incidents involving Metro railcars.

1048-1049 Collision outside Rhode Island Ave

September 11th, 1977

These four pairs are located at Guardian Centers of Georgia as part of a mock subway station & tunnel system, used for airsoft and emergency training. Two of the pairs, 1070-1071 and 1130-1131 were originally retired after being damaged in the June 22, 2009 collision outside Fort Totten. The cars were delivered to Guardian Centers in November 2012. Car 1070 is now paired with 1130, while 1071 is paired with 1131. Cars 1024 & 1025 were used to simulate a bombing or collision in a subway system. 1024 was placed on its side and 1025 was blown up in 2013 to simulate damage from an explosion. All eight cars are still present as of 2026.
