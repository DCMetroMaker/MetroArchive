Mockups

Prior to the construction of a new railcar series, multiple full-size mockups have been constructed to give an idea of what the finalized railcars will look like.

"144" - Pre-1000-Series Mockup (1968)

The first ever full-size Metro car every built. It was constructed by Sundberg-Ferar.

2000-Series Mockup (1981)

This mockup has little information, with only a single image surviving of it.

5000-Series Mockup (2000)

A single photo of this mockup exists at it's unveiling ceremony on February 3rd, 2000.

7000-Series Soft Mockup (2011)

This was the first soft mockup of the 7000-Series railcar built by WMATA to test design aspects.

"7000M" - 7000-Series Hard Mockup (2012)

The 7000-Series mockup built by Kawasaki and unveiled at the Carmen E. Turner Facility. It would be sent to Kawasaki's Lincoln, NE plant during the 7Ks construction, but was later returned to the Carmen E. Turner Facility in 2023. It was renumbered 7000M and is used as an Emergency Training Simulator.

8000-Series Soft Mockup (2024)

This was the first mockup of the 8000-Series in it's original livery. It was displayed on the National Mall in April 2024.
