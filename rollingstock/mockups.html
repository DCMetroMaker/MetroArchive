Mockups

Prior to the construction of a new railcar series, full-size mockups are constructed to give an idea of what the finalized railcars will look like.

"144" - Pre-1000-Series Mockup (1968)

The first ever full-size Metro car built. It was constructed by Sundberg-Ferar in 1968 and accepted by WMATA on August 15th, 1968. It would be transported to Washington DC for multiple public exhbitiions to showcase and promote Metrorail to the public. On September 8th, 1968 it ws unveiled on the National Mall before being moved to the White House Lawn on the 12th, where it was toured by President Lyndon B. Johnson. After that, it would be exhibited across the DMV at malls and shopping centers including Prince George's Plaza, Marlow Heights, Eastover, Tysons Corner, Landmark, Seven Corners, Turnpike & Pickett, Virginia Square, Belair, Broadale, and Wheaton Plaza. Later in 1968, it was part of the mockup station model at the future site of Rhode Island Ave Station. Its final documented public appearance was Metro's groundbreaking ceremony at Judiciary Sq on December 9th, 1969.

2000-Series Mockup (1981)

This mockup appears to have slightly thinner window frames compared to the final 2Ks and likely uses paper to simulate the new flip-dot destination sign displaying 'ROCKVILLE'. A single image survives of this mockup from a Breda advertisement.

7000-Series Soft Mockup (2011)

This was the first soft mockup of the 7000-Series railcar built by WMATA to test design aspects.

"7000M" - 7000-Series Hard Mockup (2012)

The final 7K mockup built by Kawasaki and unveiled at the Carmen E. Turner Facility. It would be sent to Kawasaki's Lincoln, NE plant during the 7Ks construction, but was later returned to the Carmen E. Turner Facility in 2023. It was renumbered 7000M and is used as an Emergency Training Simulator.

8000-Series Soft Mockup (2024)

The first mockup of the 8000-Series in its original blue, black, and silver livery. It was displayed on the National Mall in April 2024.
