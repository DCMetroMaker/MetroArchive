Retired Railcars

This page will cover every Metro railcar which has been retired from service and later survived somewhere else, rather than going straight to the scrapyard.

1024-1025, 1070-1071, 1130-1131, 1236-1237

Guardian Centers - Perry, GA

These four pairs are located at Guardian Centers of Georgia as part of a mock subway station & tunnel system, used for airsoft and emergency training. Two of the pairs, 1070-1071 and 1130-1131 were originally retired after being damaged in the June 22, 2009 collision outside Fort Totten. The cars were delivered to Guardian Centers in November 2012. Car 1070 is now paired with 1130, while 1071 is paired with 1131. Cars 1024 & 1025 were used to simulate a bombing or collision in a subway system. 1024 was placed on its side and 1025 was blown up in 2013 to simulate damage from an explosion. All 8 cars are still present as of 2025.

1090-1091

Asymmetric Warfare Training Center - Fort A.P. Hill, VA

This pair was sent to the Asymmetric Warfare Training Center around late 2013, following a fire at Silver Spring on May 14th, 2013 and subsequent derailment outside Rhode Island Ave on August 30th, 2013. The cars can be pushed across a short section of track including a station, tunnel and outdoor portion. The facility is part of the US Army's Fort A.P. Hill and was originally built and operated by the Army's Asymmetric Warfare Group. However, the Asymmetric Warfare Group inactivated in March 2021, leaving the facility rarely used by other military units. On September 16th, 2025 it was announced that the U.S. Customs and Border Protection's Special Response Team would become the new full-time tennants of the facility. The railcars are still present, stored in the underground tunnel as of 2025.

3216

Transportation Technology Center - Pueblo, CO

This railcar was damaged in a collision at West Falls Church Yard on November 29th, 2009 and was later sent to the Transportation Technology Center in Pueblo, Colorado in early 2015. There it was used for explosion testing in the blast tunnel and blown up sometime around 2023. Despite its damage, it is still on TTC property as of 2025.

4020-4021

Montgomery County Public Safety Training Academy - Montgomery Village, MD

After the retirement of the 4000-Series, this pair was donated to the Montgomery County Public Safety Training Academy to be used for training. They were delivered on August 30th, 2018 and are still present as of 2026.

4044-4045

Loudoun County Fire and Rescue Training Academy - Leesburg, VA

This pair was donated to construct Loudoun County's Fire and Rescue Metrorail Training Simulator in preparation for the opening of the Silver Line extension to Loudoun County. They were delivered on April 29th, 2017 and are still present as of 2025.

4074-4075

New York State Preparedness Training Center - Oriskany, NY

This pair was sent north to the New York State Preparedness Training Center. Notably they have gained nameplates, “Wager” on 4074 & “Turley” on 4075. They are still present as of 2025.

5058

metrobar DC - Brentwood, DC

This is the only retired Metro railcar which is open to the public and it’s still in DC! After the 5000-Series retired, 5058 & 5059 were purchased by an investor and stored at an abandoned gas station lot outside West Hyattsville from September 2019 until January 2020. Car 5058 was cut into two halves which would later be moved to the location of metrobar DC in June 2021. It later gained a unique Cherry Blossom & Pride themed livery and refurbished interior opening in 2023. It is still present as of 2026, located only a few minutes from Rhode Island Ave Station.

5059

Storage - Halethorpe, MD

This car was purchased alongside 5058, being moved together to an abandoned gas station lot outside West Hyattsville in September 2019. Then in January 2020, it was cut into two halves which were moved to a private property in Halethorpe, Maryland. It remains in storage as of 2025.

6050-6051

Carmen E. Turner Facility - Landover, MD

After being damaged in a collision at New Carrollton Yard in 2013, 6050 was sent back to Alstom's plant in Hornell, NY for repairs, eventually returning to Greenbelt Yard in 2019. It was paired with 6051 again but they did not re-enter service. Instead they remained in storage and in 2023 they were sent to the training tunnel at the Carmen E. Turner Facility in Landover, Maryland to replace 3191 & 3252. They are still present as of 2026.

5 Unknown 4000-Series Railcars

Transportation Technology Center - Pueblo, CO

Other than 3216, there are at least five other Breda railcars at the Transportation Technology Center in Pueblo, Colorado with unknown car numbers which were delivered before June 2018. They are likely 4000-Series railcars as WMATA’s 1K/4K retirement documents state that 12 4Ks were donated rather than scrapped. Out of the 12, only seven are known (4020-4021, 4044-4045, 4074-4075 & 4089), leaving five 4K railcars unaccounted for. Since five is the exact amount visible at the TTC, it seems these unknown railcars are 4088 and two other unidentified 4K pairs. However, there is also a possibility they could be 3217 (since 3216 is at the TTC) and two pairs from the June 22nd, 2009 collision, 3036-3037 & 3256-3257, as they never returned to service and have unknown fates. If you have more information or find images of these railcars send me a message! As of September 2023, Google Earth aerials only show three railcars remaining on property with the fates of the other two unknown.

1000-1001, 2000-2001, 4000-4001, 5000-5001

Greenbelt Yard - Greenbelt, MD

When the 1000-Series were retired in 2017, WMATA made the decision to retain the first pair of railcars, 1000-1001, for historical significance. This would become a tradition followed for each subsequent railcar series retired, creating WMATA's small preservation fleet.

See Preservation Fleet.

3191 & 3252 (Scrapped 2023)

Carmen E. Turner Facility - Landover, MD

Both of these cars were damaged after they collided outside Shady Grove on January 6th, 1996 when 3252 telescoped over 3191. They both sustained serious damage and would be retired from service. In 2002 they were both sent to the new training tunnel at the Carmen E. Turner Facility in Landover, Maryland. They were present until 2023, when they were removed from the tunnel and scrapped, replaced by 6050-6051. Their former mates, 3190 & 3253 were paired together and renumbered to 3290-3291, which continued to run in service until they were retired and scrapped in March 2026.

4089 (Scrapped 2017)

Grosvenor-Strathmore Station - Rockville, MD

After retirement, 4089 was cut into multiple segments for a pilot pop-up shop display outside Grosvenor-Strathmore Station in Summer 2017. They were removed in October 2017 and likely scrapped. The rear segment was not used for the pop-up shop and still survives on private property as of 2026.
