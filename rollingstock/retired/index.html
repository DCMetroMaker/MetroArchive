Guardian Centers - Perry, Georgia

TBD TBD The first two railcars preserved by WMATA, 1000-1001 were the start of the preservation fleet in 2017. It would begin to undergo some sort of restoration process around 2017-2018 which involved gutting the interior, stripping the paint off the exterior and giving the bulkheads a white primer. It restoration would be seemingly paused and it returned to storage by late 2018 in a rough state. the 1K would remain in storge alongside the 4K & 5K until late 2025 when on October 1st, Metro's GM Randy Clarke posted a picture of 1000-1001 in Greenbelt Yard's Paint/Body Shop, teasing that restoration had resumed.
