Preservation Fleet

When the 1000-Series were retired in 2017, WMATA made the decision to retain the first pair of railcars, 1000-1001, for historical significance. This would become a tradition followed for each subsequent railcar fleet retired. The preservation fleet consists of eight railcars and is typically stored on storage track 4 in Greenbelt Yard, not publically viewable outside of special events. The cars are not regularly maintained or operated while in storage and are in varying states of disuse.

1000-1001

The first two railcars preserved by WMATA, 1000-1001 were the start of the preservation fleet in 2017. They would begin to undergo a minor restoration process around 2018 which involved gutting the interior, stripping the paint off the exterior and giving the bulkheads a white primer. It's restoration would be seemingly paused and it returned to storage by late 2018 in a rough state. The 1K would remain in storge alongside the 4K & 5K until late 2025 when on October 1st, Metro's GM Randy Clarke posted a picture of 1000-1001 in Greenbelt Yard's Paint Shop, teasing that restoration had resumed. It would be confirmed on June 11th, 2026 that Car 1000 had been restored to be displayed on the National Mall as part of America's 250th celebrations. Car 1000 would be delivered by truck to the Mall the night of June 14th, on display to the public for the first time in almost a decade. Car 1001 was not restored and remained unpainted in Greenbelt Yard.

2000-2001

The most recent addition to WMATA's preservation fleet, 2000-2001 is the only one to have a preservation run after it's official retirement. It last ran as part of the 2K Last Ride event on October 19th, 2024. In storage, it still wears the 2K Last Ride wrap & decals as of 2026. Other than Money Train cars 2016-2017, which will likely be scrapped upon retirement, 2000-2001 is the only surviving pair of 2Ks.

4000-4001

Alongside 1000-1001, 4000-4001 were also retained following the 4000-Series retirement in 2017. They would be placed in storage and have been left mostly untouched since. The left-side middle door on 4000 has been partially open since 2021 for unknown reasons.

5000-5001

After the retirement of the 5Ks in 2018, 5000-5001 joined the preservation fleet. They were temporarily moved in September 2025 to get the 1K out of storage.
