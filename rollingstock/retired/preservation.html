Preservation Fleet

When the 1000-Series were retired in 2017, WMATA made the decision to retain the first pair of railcars, 1000-1001, for historical significance. This would become a tradition followed for each subsequent railcar fleet retired. The preservation fleet is typically stored on storage track 4 in Greenbelt Yard and is not publically viewable. The cars are not regularly maintained or operated and are in varying states of disuse.

2000-2001

The most recent addition to WMATA's preservation fleet, 2000-2001 is the only one to have a preservation run after it's official retirement. It last ran as part of the 2K Last Ride event on October 19th, 2024. In storage, it still wears the 2K Last Ride wrap & decals as of 2026.
