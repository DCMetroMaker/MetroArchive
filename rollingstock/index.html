Rolling Stock

Metro has operated a wide range of rolling stock for various purposes from passeneger service to track maintenance.

Image Rosters

Images of (almost) every railcar in Metro's current fleet!

Metrorail's Fleet

The most iconic aspect of Metro's Rolling Stock, all of the passenger trains old and new. Also referred to as Class 1 Vehicles.

Non-Revenue Vehicles

The vehicles which operate behind-the-scenes to keep the Metro system running safely and efficiently. Also referred to as Class 2 Vehicles.

Retired Railcars

Railcars which survived in some form following their retirement from passneger service in the Metro system. They've travelled as far as Colorado, New York, and Georgia.

Railcar Wraps

Starting in 2005, WMATA began decorating train cars with sponsored advertising wraps. In addition, seasonal holiday & event wraps have been a yearly tradition since Winter 2022.

Mockups

Prior to the construction of a new railcar series, multiple mockups have been constructed to give an idea of what the finalized railcars will look like.
