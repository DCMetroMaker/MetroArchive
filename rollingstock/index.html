Rolling Stock

Metro has operated a wide range of rolling stock for various purposes from passeneger service to track maintenance.

Metrorail's Fleet

The most iconic aspect of Metro's rolling stock. All of the passenger trains, old and new. Also referred to as Class 1 Vehicles.

Non-Revenue Vehicles

The vehicles which operate behind-the-scenes, typically at night or during closures, to keep the Metro system running safely and efficiently. Also referred to as Class 2 Vehicles.

Retired Railcars

Railcars which found a second life after their retirement from the Metro system and still survive in some form. They've travelled as far as Colorado, New York, and Georgia.

Railcar Wraps

Railcars can be wrapped for a variety of purposes. Starting in 2005, WMATA began featuring train cars with sponsored advertising wraps. In addition, seasonal holiday & event wraps have become a yearly tradition since Winter 2022.

Mockups

Prior to the construction of a new railcar series, prototype mockups are constructed to give an idea of what the finalized railcars will look like.
