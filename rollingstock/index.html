Rolling Stock

Over the past 50 years, Metro's rail fleet has included 1888 railcars from seven numbered series, built by five different manufacturers. Their current fleet consists of 1176 3K, 6K, & 7K railcars.

Image Rosters

On May 3rd, 1972, WMATA awarded a $91.6 million contract to Rohr Industries to construct and deliver the first 300 Metro railcars. These cars would be known as the 1000-Series (1Ks) and were constructed at Rohr’s plant in Winder, Georgia. The pilot pair (1000-1001) was delivered by railroad to WMATA’s Brentwood Yard on November 16th, 1974. The 1000-Series first entered service on Metro’s opening day, March 27th, 1976, with around 40 of the 300 cars delivered at the time.
