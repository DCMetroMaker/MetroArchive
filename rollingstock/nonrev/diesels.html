L001

A General Electric 44-Ton Switcher likely built for the US Army. Its history before WMATA is currently unknown, but based on its design it may have been built in the mid-1940s. After being aquired by WMATA around the 1980s, it was renumbered 846. It had a black livery with yellow handrails and would be rebuilt with a lowered drop cab to fit Metro's tunnel clearances. Shortly after, it would be renumbered again to L001 and gain red/white/blue stripes along its front and rear ends. After 2007, it would be left to decay in Greenbelt Yard along with L011, before being scrapped around late 2010.

L002

A General Electric 44-Ton Switcher built in February 1953 for the US Air Force, originally numbered USAF 1238 (GE #31876). It was stationed at Laughlin Air Force Base in Del Rio, TX. In 1974, it was sold or donated to WMATA for the construction of Metrorail, being renumbered 865. It was rebuilt in October 1974, with a lowered drop cab and an all-yellow livery, with a black Metro logo and number on its cab sides. It was later renumbered again to L002. By 2016, it would be repainted into a new livery, with a white base and blue wing patterns on each end. Its number and "Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority" were painted in blue on its sides, along with a white Metro logo on its front and rear vents. It would be auctioned off and sold to Ray's Transportation in New Windsor, NY in 2023. It kept most of its existing livery, with new "Ray's Transportation - New Windsor" text in black.

L011

A General Electric 45-Ton Drop Cab built in October 1944 for the US Army Transportation Corps, European Theater of Operations, originally numbered 8569 (GE #27707). It was shipped to France for World War II on November 13th, 1944 and returned to the US in May 1945. Later would be transferred to Spokane Air Force Base (Now Fairchild AFB) and kept its number as USAF 8569. Transferred again to the US Navy, where it became USN 65-00527 at St. Julien's Creek Naval Annex. It would be sold or donated to WMATA in the early 1970s for the construction of Metrorail. It was renumbered to 860 and painted in a white livery with orange railings and red/white/blue stripes along the cab and front/rear of the locomotive. By the 1980s, it was renumbered again to L011. After 2007, it would be left to decay in Greenbelt Yard along with L001, before being scrapped around late 2010.

L012

A General Electric 45-Ton Drop Cab built for the US Army Transportation Corps, European Theater of Operations. Its original US Army number is unknown, so its history before WMATA is currently lost. Once aquired by WMATA it was renumbered L012 and painted in a completely yellow livery, with a black Metro logo and number on its cab sides. It would be scrapped at an unknown date after 1995.
