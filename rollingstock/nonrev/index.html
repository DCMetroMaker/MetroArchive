Non-Revenue Vehicles

Vehicles operated by WMATA that aren't used for passenger/revenue service.

Work Vehicles

Work vehicles painted in yellow are commonly seen during closures or single tracking used for maintenance.

Money Train

The Money Train travels around the system on most weekdays to collect cash from station's fare revenue machines.

Feeler Car

The Feeler Car (also known as the Clearance Car) was used to measure tunnel clearances around the system.

Center Cab Diesels

From the late 1970s when Metro was under construction, WMATA owned a set of five 44-ton Center Cab Diesel Electric locomotives. All five would be slowly retired over the following decades.
