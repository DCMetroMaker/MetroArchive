Money Train

The Money Train, also known as Revenue Collection Vehicles (RCVs), travels around the system on most weekdays to collect cash from station's fare revenue machines. They operate out of Alexandria Yard, where WMATA's Revenue Collection Facility is located.

Current

2016-2017

Following the conversion of 6182-6183, this pair was removed from service and converted to revenue collection beginning around July 2018. The cars recieved a yellow wrap and were mostly gutted, with modifications to carry cash transport carts. In February 2020, it began revenue collection operations, replacing the final four 1K Money Train cars, 8002-8003 and 8004-8005. The yellow wrap would be replaced with a silver wrap by December 2023.

6182-6183

This pair was removed from service and converted to revenue collection beginning around June 2017. The cars recieved a yellow wrap and were mostly gutted, with modifications to carry cash transport carts. In May 2018, it began revenue collection operations, replacing Money Train cars 8000-8001. The yellow wrap would appear to be damaged in November 2023, before being replaced with a silver wrap by December 2023.

Past

8000-8001

Formerly 1010-1011. This pair was renumbered and converted to Money Train operations in the 1990s. It was mechanically rehabilitated and retained its front mylar rollsigns, displaying 'Special', while its side rollsigns were removed, replaced by large 'NO PASSENGERS' text. It would be scrapped on April 4th, 2018, replaced by Money Train cars 6182-6183.

8002-8003

Formerly 1044-1045. This pair was renumbered and converted to Money Train operations in the 1990s. It was mechanically rehabilitated, but still retained a single original mylar rollsign per car side. Starting in 2018, it would only be run in the center of a 6-car train. It would be scrapped in early 2020, replaced by Money Train cars 2016-2017.

8004-8005

Formerly 1092-1093. This pair was renumbered and converted to Money Train operations in 2016. Starting in 2018, it would only be run in the center of a 6-car train. After only 4 years in Money Train service, it would be scrapped in early 2020, replaced by Money Train cars 2016-2017.
