Money Train

The Money Train, also known as Revenue Collection Vehicles (RCVs), travels around the system on most weekdays to collect cash from station's fare revenue machines. They operate out of Alexandria Yard, where WMATA's Revenue Collection Facility is located.

Current

2016-2017

This pair was first converted to Money Train operations between July 2018 and February 2020. Unlike the previous 1K Money Train, it would not be renumbered.

6182-6183

This pair was removed from service and converted to revenue collection beginning around June 2017. The cars recieved a yellow wrap and were mostly gutted, with modifications to carry cash transport carts. In July 2018, it began revenue collection operations, replacing Money Train cars 8000-8001.

Past

8000-8001

Formerly 1010-1011. This pair was renumbered and converted to Money Train operations in the 1990s. It was never rehabilitated and retained the distinctive vinyl rollsigns, always displaying 'NO PASSENGERS' on the sides and 'Special' on the front bulkheads. It would be scrapped on April 4th, 2018.

8002-8003

Formerly 1044-1045. This pair was renumbered and converted to Money Train operations in the 1990s. It would be scrapped in early 2020.

8004-8005

Formerly 1092-1093. This pair was renumbered and converted to Money Train operations in 2016. This was the only rehabilitated 1K to be part of the Money Train. It would be scrapped in early 2020, after only 4 years in Money Train service.
