Feeler Car

1028

This car's mate, 1029, was destroyed in the January 13th, 1982 derailment between Federal Triangle and Smithsonian. It would be modified into the Feeler Car around the late 1980s. It was no longer operable without a pair, so its direction was switched, allowing it to be moved by a diesel or prime mover. The typical brown bulkhead became the rear end and the gray end became the front. On its new front end it gained a headlight above the door and three sets of feelers, front, middle, and rear. Since it was never rehabilitated it retained original vinyl rollsigns, with the front-right displaying "Special" and rear-left displaying "No Passengers". The rear bulkhead door was switched to tan and the rear-right Metro logo was removed or covered for unknown reasons. Car 1028 would be retired and scrapped on April 5th, 2016.

Other Feelers

Prior to the conversion of 1028 to permanent Feeler Car in the late 80s, other 1K cars served as the Feeler Car.
