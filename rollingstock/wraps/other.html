Vinyl Wraps

Starting in 2017, WMATA began a pilot trial wrapping multiple 3000-Series & 6000-Series railcars all in silver to look like 7000-Series railcars. Seven 3K pairs & only one 6K pair would be wrapped in total by 2018. They all remain wrapped as of 2026.
