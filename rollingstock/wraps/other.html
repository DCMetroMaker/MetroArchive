Other Wraps

Additional examples of wraps on railcars.

Silver Vinyl Wraps

Starting in 2017, WMATA began a pilot trial wrapping multiple 3000-Series & 6000-Series railcars all in silver to look like 7000-Series railcars. Seven 3K pairs & only one 6K pair would be wrapped in total by 2018. They all remain wrapped as of 2026.

2K Last Ride Wrap

Following the 2000-Series retirement in May 2024, WMATA held a retirement run known as the 2K Last Ride on October 19th, 2024. Three 2K pairs, including the to be preserved 2000-2001, were cleaned up and wrapped to appear as shiny brand new on the exterior. The wrap would only reach the top of the brown stripe, but also include the front bulkheads. They also recieved 2K Last Ride decals between the cab windows and first set of doors. 2038-2039 & 2058-2059 would be scrapped wearing this wrap, while 2000-2001 still features the wrap in storage.

Legacy Vinyl Wraps

At some point before 2024, WMATA began wrapping some 3Ks in a wrap which matched the standard legacy fleet livery. It appears more are still being wrapped as of 2025, so this list is incomplete.
