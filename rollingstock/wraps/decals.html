Railcar Decals

In addition to wraps, sometimes railcars gain commemorative decals to mark events or milestones. This list does not include warning or emergency decals.

10th Anniversary Decal (1986)

To celebrate Metrorail's 10th Anniversary in 1986, at least one railcar recieved decals featuring the 10th Anniversary logo to the left of the front boarding door. A single image from a vintage Metro postcard shows the decal featured on car 1004.

American Flag Decal (1991)

Beginning around 1991, railcars gained American Flag decals on their front bulkhead cab doors, below the window. Most would be removed by 2001 when the 25th Anniversary decals were placed in the same spot, but some surived until as late as 2007. The first Money Train pair, 8000-8001, would feature this decal up until their retirement in 2018.

1K 25th Anniversary Decal (2001)

To celebrate Metrorail's 25th Anniversary in 2001, many railcars would recieve metallic silver decals on their front bulkhead cab doors featuring the 25th Anniversary logo. On any 1000-Series railcars, the decal was unique. Curved around the top of the logo was text reading "CLASSIC CAR", and placed below the logo was a short sentence denoting the historic status of the railcar. It read: "This is one of the original Rohr Metro cars". Over the years many of the decals would be removed, but some survived up until the 1Ks retirement in 2017.

2K/3K/4K 25th Anniversary Decal (2001)

This is the standard version of the metallic silver 25th Anniversary decal, placed on the front bulkhead cab doors of 2Ks, 3Ks, and 4Ks during 2001. It did not feature any additional text unlike the 1K variant, just the 25th Anniversary logo in the center. During the 2K's & 3K's rehabilitation, which began the following year in 2002, all of their 25th Anniversary decals were removed. The 4Ks did not undergo rehabilitation and many of their decals survived until their retirement in 2017. Cars 4020-4021 still feature the decal post-retirement at the Montgomery County Public Safety Training Academy, albeit with major sun fading.

American Flag Decal V2 (2002)

To observe and honor one year since the Septemeber 11th attacks, Metro cars wore American flag decals on their front bulkhead cab doors. These decals featured the same flag artwork as the 1991 design, but were semi-transparent and placed in the bulkhead door's center window. Most would be removed from 2Ks & 3Ks during their rehabilitation, while some decals on 1Ks, 4Ks, and 5Ks would survive until their retirement in 2017 and 2018. However, a few 3Ks still feature this flag decal to this day: 3090, 3113, 3263, & 3278.

American Flag Decal V3 (2025)

Starting in March 2025, railcars began recieving a pair of American Flag decals above either their front-most or rear-most boarding doors in preparration for America's 250th Anniversary in 2026. This placement would later be revised to the upper-right of the middle boarding door. All active 3Ks, 6Ks, and 7Ks would receive these decals, as well as the 2016-2017 & 6182-6183 Money Train pairs.

APTA Transit Agency of the Year Decal (2025)

To celebrate WMATA winning APTA's 2025 Transit Agency of the Year award, a few railcars would gain commemerative decals to the left of the middle boarding door. It is currently featured on cars 3070-3071, 6026-6027, 6082-6083, & 7150-7151.
