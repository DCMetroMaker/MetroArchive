Seasonal Wraps

Starting in Winter 2022, WMATA began a tradition of wrapping a train of cars to celebrate a seasonal holiday, event or award. The yearly wraps typically follow the pattern: Holiday, Cherry Blossom, Pride Month, July 4th, Veterans Day; with some additional wraps.

Year 1 Seasonal Wraps (2022-2023)

The first two seasonal wraps were on 7000-Series railcars, next two on 3000-Series railcars, and final two on 6000-Series railcars. They do not wrap the M Logo on 7K A-Cars. Since July 2023, all seasonal wraps have been on 6000-Series railcars.
