Seasonal Wraps

Starting in Winter 2022, WMATA began a tradition of wrapping a train of cars to celebrate a seasonal holiday, event or award.

7000-Series Seasonal Wraps (2022-2023)

The first two seasonal wraps were on 7000-Series railcars. They do not wrap the M Logo on 7K cars.

Holiday 2022 Wrap (December 2022)

Silver Line Wrap (January 2023)

3000-Series Seasonal Wraps (2023)

The next two seasonal wraps were on 3000-Series railcars.

Cherry Blossom 2023 Wrap (March 2023)

Pride Month 2023 Wrap (June 2023)

6000-Series Seasonal Wraps (2023-Present)

Since July 2023, all seasonal wraps have been on 6000-Series railcars.

July 4th 2023 Wrap (July 2023)

Veterans Day 2023 Wrap (November 2023)

Holiday 2023 Wrap (December 2023)

Cherry Blossom 2024 Wrap (March 2024)

Pride Month 2024 Wrap (June 2024)
