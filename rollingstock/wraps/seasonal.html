Seasonal Wraps

Starting in Winter 2022, WMATA began a tradition of wrapping a train of cars to celebrate a seasonal holiday, event or award.

7000-Series Seasonal Wraps (2022-2023)

The first two seasonal wraps were on 7000-Series railcars. They do not wrap the M Logo on 7K cars.
