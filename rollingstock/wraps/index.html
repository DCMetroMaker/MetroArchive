Railcar Wraps

Metro wraps train cars for seasonal holidays and sponsored events or products.

Seasonal Wraps

Starting in Winter 2022, WMATA began a tradition of wrapping a train of cars to celebrate a seasonal holiday, event or award.

Advertising Wraps

Advertisers can pay to have limited-time sponsored advertisements on the sides of railcars.

Other Wraps

Additional wraps which are for pilot tests, one-off events or non-revenue vehicles.

Decals

Commemerative decals applied to railcars to mark special events or milestones.
